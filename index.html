How my mind works

I see the world a little differently. My mind is full of ideas, questions, and possibilities. I'm naturally curious, and when something catches my interest, I want to understand how it works, explore it, and discover what I can create with it.

Living with ADHD has its challenges, especially when it comes to staying organized, maintaining focus, and turning every idea into a finished project. But through learning, experimenting, and building things, I'm gradually discovering ways to work with my mind rather than against it.

I'm learning to break complex ideas into smaller steps, approach problems from different angles, and turn my curiosity into practical skills. Whether I'm exploring ICT, learning to code, or working on a new project, every experience teaches me something new.

For me, development isn't just about writing code. It's about asking questions, solving problems, exploring possibilities, and creating things that can make a difference.

I'm still learning how my mind works, still improving my skills, and still turning ideas into reality — one project at a time.

That's the journey behind NØX.DEV.
